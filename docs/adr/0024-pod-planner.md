# ADR 0024: Pod planner and Gardyn plant catalog

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-11-02

## Context

`lightZones.ts` shows each pod's light zone and says when a planted pod
would suit its plant better elsewhere. That helps one pod at a time. It does
not answer the questions you have before planting a batch of yCubes: where
should these twelve go, and should they all start today?

Gardyn publishes the answers in prose: each plant's zone, "if the ideal spot
is unavailable, place your plants one light zone lower", cucumbers at the
bottom of the middle column, space between large plants, and a succession
table that staggers sowing by crop group. A third-party web app, My Pod
Planner, automates this but has no licence and departs from Gardyn's rules in
places (knowledge/evaluation/mypodplanner-crosscheck-2026-11-02.md).

## Decision

- **Catalog** (`src/plants/catalog.json`, generated by
  `scripts/plants/build-catalog.py`): 139 plants from Gardyn's zone guide,
  with first-harvest windows, care level and yield from Gardyn's yCube product
  pages. Fields derived by rule, not published by Gardyn, carry
  `harvestEstimated` or `successionInferred`
  (knowledge/plants/catalog-provenance.md). A test keeps every zone equal to
  `PLANT_ZONES` in `lightZones.ts`.
- **One grid.** The planner reads pod zones from `lightZones.ts`; it has no
  grid of its own. The nursery (10 pods, no zones) is the only planner-only
  model.
- **Layout** (`src/plants/planner.ts`) follows Gardyn's rules literally: ideal
  zone, else step down one zone, never up; never substitute another plant; on
  a Home, cucumbers take the bottom of the middle column; large plants avoid
  neighbouring large plants. What does not fit is reported, not dropped.
- **Sowing**: the next yCube of a plant starts at the previous one's
  first-harvest month plus its succession group's gap (10, 21, 31 or 56
  days); flowers and "all now" mode sow together.
- **Plans** are stored (`plans` table). Applying one marks today's pods
  seeded, notes later waves on their pods, starts a grow cycle if none is
  open, and the grow engine raises `reminder_sow` when each later wave is due.
- Exposed on the API (`/api/planner/*`, `/api/plans`), as MCP tools
  (`plant_catalog`, `planner_zones`, `plan_layout`, `best_spot`, `save_plan`,
  `apply_plan`), on the Plan screen, and as a Home Assistant reminder.
- **Explaining what did not fit** (#127): every plant gets its ideal zone
  before any plant falls back, and a fallback is exactly one zone lower. A
  layout carries a per-zone summary (rows, slots, planted, planned, free); each
  unplaced plant carries a code, the zones it could use and the pods in them;
  and suggestions name a pod to relocate to a slot its plant tolerates, or
  offer to allow one zone further down. Callers can pin pods to slots or set a
  plant's allowed zones; each suggestion carries the overrides that carry it
  out, and a saved plan keeps the overrides it was computed with.
- **Custom plants** (#130): crops missing from Gardyn's list can be added on
  the unit (name, category, light zone, first-harvest window, care level,
  size; the succession group defaults from the category). They live in the
  `custom_plants` table, so they travel in the database backup (ADR 0021),
  and are merged after the built-in plants for the catalog, layouts, best
  spots and sowing. Built-in plants stay read-only. Exposed as
  `/api/planner/catalog` POST/PATCH/DELETE, the MCP tools `add_plant`,
  `update_plant` and `remove_plant`, and "Add plant" on the Plan screen.
- **Plan mode over MCP** (#126): `match_order` turns pasted order lines
  ("Genovese Basil x2") into plant ids and counts. It compares weighted word
  sets (distinctive words count more than shared ones like "pepper"), accepts
  near-miss spellings and a few common synonyms, and returns a confidence per
  line, close alternatives, and the lines it could not match with
  suggestions. A line naming a colour or variety a plant lacks ("Pink Swiss
  Chard" against Red Swiss Chard) never matches that plant: it comes back
  unmatched with the plant as a suggestion and a `reason`. `list_plans`, `get_plan`, `update_plan` and `remove_plan`
  manage saved plans. A plan serves its first sow date, and the Plan screen's
  "Open" reloads a plan's plants, overrides and start date. Because the planner
  is deterministic, the screen then shows the layout and schedule that were
  saved, and saving updates the same plan.

## Consequences

- Catalog harvest windows are months; ADR 0022's per-crop profiles stay the
  place for day-level windows and leaf-cover targets. A later change could
  seed profiles from the catalog.
- The catalog goes stale as Gardyn's range changes: re-extract, rebuild and
  review the diff.
- Applying a plan overwrites the pods it names; the MCP tool says to confirm
  with the user first.
- Not built: an automatic plant mix from preferences. If added, it should come
  from Gardyn's kit guidance rather than invented ratios.
