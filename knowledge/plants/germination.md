# Germination

From Gardyn's help centre. Days count from sowing, as in [Watering schedules](../watering/schedules.md).

## Before germinating

Always remove the cardboard covers before germinating the yCubes. Germination does best at around 70°F with 65–75% humidity; too cool, too dry or too humid can slow it or stop it. The Gardyn app shows the readings under Settings → Advanced.

## Sprouting

Check the yCubes daily for signs of sprouting. Some seeds take up to 21 days to sprout; the Plant Book gives each plant's time. Too much water can oversaturate the seed and slow or prevent sprouting.

## Thinning and yCovers

Place yCovers only after thinning the sprouts: covers can trap excess moisture around the seed.
