# Catalog provenance

`src/plants/catalog.json` is generated by `scripts/plants/build-catalog.py` from raw extracts kept next to it, read on 2026-11-02 (the Plant Book and Iowa State's germination table on 2026-11-17):

| Field | Source | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| `zone` | Gardyn's placement-by-zone guide | all 139 (138 guide lines; "Dragon Beans Green Beans" split into two plants) |
| `harvestMonths` | "First Harvest" on each yCube product page | 69 published; the rest use the category median and set `harvestEstimated` |
| `careLevel`, `yield` | "Care Level", "Yield" on product pages | 72 (store items only; null otherwise) |
| `inStore`, `gardynHandle` | yCube collection | 73 sold today |
| `succession` | Gardyn's succession table groups | exact when named there, else by category with `successionInferred` |
| `sproutDays` | "Days to sprout" on each plant's Plant Book page (`gardyn-plant-book-sprout.txt`); for plants with no Plant Book page, "Days to Germination" for their crop in Iowa State Extension's germination table (`isu-germination.txt`); both read 2026-11-17 | 104 (52 Plant Book, 52 Iowa State; [which plants](#sprouting-windows)), kept beside the plants by id; `categorySproutMedians` stands in for the other 35 (`sproutWindow` flags it estimated) |
| `category`, `size`, `placement` | rules in the build script | `size: large` = fruiting/vining or sunflower; `placement` = cucumber exception |
| `tolerates` | rules in `src/plants/tolerance.ts`, applied when the catalog loads | by category and zone ([zone tolerance](zone-tolerance.md)); not in `catalog.json` |

No prose is copied: care notes are not in the catalog. Microgreens are excluded (Gardyn grows them in trays, not pods).

## Sprouting windows

The Plant Book comes first. On 2026-11-17 every plant without a Plant Book window was re-checked at `mygardyn.com/plant-book/<id>/`: each link 404s, lands on a yCube product page (no days to sprout), or lands on a family page with no days (`grow-flowers`, `grow-fruits`, `plants`). Bull's Blood Beets still lands on the kale page, so its Plant Book figure isn't used.

Iowa State's windows are usually tighter than the Plant Book's for the same crop (kale 4–7 against Gardyn's 7–21, peppers 7–10 against 10–28), so those plants may show "late?" sooner.

## Known gaps

- Harvest windows are months, not days; the sowing planner converts at 30 days per month.
- Succession gaps use the midpoint of Gardyn's ranges (10, 21, 31, 56 days); flowers have no fixed cadence.
- Gardyn's products change; re-run the extraction and the build script, then review the diff.
