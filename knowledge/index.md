# seedypod knowledge

Evidence and decisions that are not code: hardware facts, horticulture targets,
calibration protocols, and later computer-vision evaluation. All initial
concepts are agent-authored drafts; no human review is claimed. Run
`python scripts/validate_knowledge.py` before committing.

- [Hardware](hardware/index.md) — Gardyn board facts and upstream failure modes.
- [Operations](operations/index.md) — Safety limits and their provenance.
- [Nutrients](nutrients/index.md) — Feeding doses, pH and EC targets, from Gardyn and from owners.
- [Plants](plants/index.md) — The light-zone grid, measured, and where the plant catalog's fields come from.
- [Evaluation](evaluation/index.md) — Dated comparisons against outside tools.
- [References](references/index.md) — Pinned OKF specification and source provenance.
