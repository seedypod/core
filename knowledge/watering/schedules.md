# Watering schedules

Gardyn doesn't publish a watering cadence: its Smart Watering adjusts the pump to the plants. Apart from germination, which follows Gardyn's germination mode, the cadences below are what owners on r/Gardyn report running, gathered as starting points, not Gardyn's guidance; adjust them by the roots. Times are the unit's local time. Days count from sowing, day 0, on the unit's calendar: a new day starts at its local midnight.

## Germination

Days 0–7, for every crop. Gardyn's germination mode (https://help.mygardyn.com/en/articles/4435265) runs one 15 min cycle on the sowing day and the next about a week later, day 7: as a plain schedule, Once a week, 15 min at 08:00, on the sowing weekday. Germination lasts through day 7, so that second run happens before the seedlings' schedule takes over on day 8. Sown after 08:00, the sowing day's run has already gone by: water the pods by hand that day. With Follow the grow cycle on, the weekly run keeps to the sowing weekday, moving if the grow cycle is restarted on another day. The lights keep their own schedule.

## Seedlings

Days 8–13. Once a day, 10 min (5–15 min), at 08:00, after the second germination cycle while the first leaves open. Fruiting crops go from germination straight to their own schedule.

## Young plants

Days 14–21. Twice a day, 6 min (5–8 min), at 08:00 and 16:00. Start at once a day for 5 min and move to two or three times a day as the roots grow.

## Mature greens and herbs

Day 22 on. Three times a day, 6 min (5–8 min), at 08:00, 14:00 and 20:00.

## Large or fruiting plants

Tomatoes, cucumbers, peppers, from day 8. Five times a day, 5 min, every 4 hours while the lights are on: 06:00, 10:00, 14:00, 18:00 and 22:00. With a shorter light day, keep only the runs inside it.

## Judging a schedule

- Check the roots. Dry, brittle roots want another cycle or a longer one; roots sitting in pooled water just before the next cycle want less.
- Space the cycles across the light hours, so pods don't dry out under the LEDs.
- Many growers switch Gardyn's smart watering off after germination, for a schedule they can predict.
