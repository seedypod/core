# Community nutrients

Owner reports, not instructions. Counts are distinct Reddit authors in the r/Gardyn archive (searched locally, 2026-11-09). One account affiliated with a reseller of Hydroponic Research products wrote 62 of the 149 Veg+Bloom comments; it is excluded from every count. Comment links are `https://www.reddit.com/r/Gardyn/comments/<post>/_/<comment>`, shown as `post/comment`.

## The products

Guaranteed analysis from labels and retailer listings, not manufacturer pages.

| Product | N-P-K | For | Owners reporting |
|---|---|---|---|
| General Hydroponics MaxiGro | 10-5-14 (Ca 6 %) | Vegetative, leafy growth | ~13 |
| General Hydroponics MaxiBloom | 5-15-14 | Flowering, fruiting | 1 (+1 suggesting) |
| Hydroponic Research Veg+Bloom, Tap/Hard or RO/Soft | 8-5-13 | Whole grow, one part; variant chosen by water hardness | ~30 |
| General Hydroponics Flora series | three-part | Ratio changed by stage | ~6 |
| Masterblend 4-18-38 + Epsom salt + calcium nitrate | mixed by the grower | Whole grow | ~7 |

MaxiGro and MaxiBloom carry the same potassium; the difference is nitrogen (leaf growth) against phosphorus (flowers and fruit). For a tower of greens and herbs, MaxiGro alone matches the crop.

Community claims, not confirmed with Gardyn: Gardyn Plant Food is Veg+Bloom Tap/Hard ("Plant food is the TAP/HARD version of this", `1ja3zdl/mhikw2l`, 2025-03-13), and HydroBoost is Hydroponic Research PHLO. RO/Soft is mostly bought by mistake.

## Doses owners use on a Gardyn

Home is 5 gal full, Studio 4 gal.

| Product | Reported dose | Note |
|---|---|---|
| MaxiGro | 1 tsp/gal (Studio) | pH read 3.9 just after mixing powder straight into the tank (`1xe912b`, 2026-10-24); the only dose reported |
| Veg+Bloom Tap/Hard | 1/2 tsp/gal (3 owners), 1 tsp/gal (2) | "For each gallon, i add 1/2 tsp veg+ bloom" (`1m7bqml/n5dfil4`, 2025-07-27) |
| VBX + PHLO | 3/4–1 tsp VBX + 1/4 tsp PHLO per gal | `1s2wxg0/ocbram8`, 2026-03-25; 1 Tbsp/gal VBX browned lettuce edges |
| Flora Gro + Micro | ~1 Tbsp each per gal on top-ups | high; another owner runs Flora at EC 1.0 |
| Masterblend A/B stocks | 4 mL/L of each stock | `1gkmgfv/lvmuqcv`, 2024-11-06 |
| CalMag (Botanicare, Bloom City) | 1 tsp/gal (~4 owners); 1/2 tsp/gal; 5 mL/gal for fruiting | "dose calmag up to 300 EC. Then liquid plant food up to 1300 EC" (`1g95ub8/ltroqw5`, RO water) |
| pH Down (PHLO, GH pH Control Kit) | PHLO 1 mL/gal to 1 tsp/gal, 5 mL/gal on hard water; GH pH Down ~1/2 tsp per Studio refresh | "That stuff is potent so take it very slow" (`1oxw9as/np0ax48`) |

Teaspoon doses are not interchangeable between products. Where an EC probe is fitted, dose to an EC target instead.

## Targets owners report

| Measure | Reported | Owners |
|---|---|---|
| pH | 5.5–6.5; those who meter aim for 5.9–6.2 | ~6 |
| EC, leafy greens | 1.3–1.6 mS/cm | 4 |
| EC, fruiting | 2.0–2.4 mS/cm (often written "2000 EC", meaning µS/cm) | 5 |
| EC at Gardyn's stock dose | "around 1200 ec" (`1pyqw8j/nwximfv`, 2025-12-31); another measured 660 µS/cm and saw lettuce tip burn | 2 |

These sit inside Gardyn's blog ranges ([Gardyn feeding guidance](gardyn-feeding-guidance.md)). A large group never tests: "I have NEVER checked pH. My yields are good." (`1rpdl3t/o9llzoj`, 2026-03-10).

Meters: 42 owners mention one; VIVOSUN is named most (11), then Apera (2), cheap pH/EC combo pens, drops and strips.

## Failure modes

| Failure | Reports |
|---|---|
| pH crash below 4 | powder plus PHLO (`1jtwi7r`, partly a miscalibrated meter); MaxiGro (`1xe912b`); algae in open pods |
| pH won't drop or springs back | pH 8.2 source water stayed near 7 at 4 mL/gal PHLO (`1rb1tgo`); back to 8–8.5 a day after GH pH Down (`1rpdl3t`) |
| Overshoot | too much pH Down took a tank to 4.5 (`1hzrpyd`) |
| Scale in tube joints | with Veg+Bloom; one owner saw much less after switching to MaxiGro (`1p3u8kh/nq7weli`), another after replacing HydroBoost with CalMag (`1oxg6pa/noz4oif`) |
| Powder slow to dissolve | Gardyn food, and MaxiGro for one owner, whose pH 3.9 settled after pre-dissolving (`1xe912b`); another finds MaxiGro dissolves better than Veg+Bloom (`1p3u8kh/nq7weli`). Dissolve in a jug first |
| Leaf-edge burn | overdose (VBX at 1 Tbsp/gal; strawberries on MaxiGro) |

## Maintainer report

The seedypod maintainer grows leafy greens on MaxiGro and finds it works well for that phase (reported 2026-11-09, firsthand, not measured).

## Gaps

- Every dose and target above is self-reported, mostly without stated water hardness or meter calibration.
- No owner reports a measured EC for MaxiGro at a given dose on a Gardyn. A tank with the EC probe fitted can record one.
- Not Gardyn: one MaxiGro lettuce report and one Masterblend report came from LetPot units and are left out above.
